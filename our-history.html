Our History

Las Vegas Warehouse was founded by veteran logistics operators who saw a gap in the market for storage and fulfillment that actually treats your inventory like it matters.

Our Story

Built on 30+ years of industry experience

Our founders spent decades in logistics and warehousing before starting Las Vegas Warehouse — long enough to know exactly what goes wrong at other facilities: damaged goods, missed shipments, and inventory nobody can find when you need it.

For more than 20 years in business, we've built our reputation on the opposite approach: treat every item, from a pallet of retail inventory to a museum-bound sculpture, like it's our own. That philosophy has earned the trust of clients ranging from major brands like Red Bull, Raising Cane's, Formula 1, DHL, and The Wynn, to private collectors and local businesses across Las Vegas.
