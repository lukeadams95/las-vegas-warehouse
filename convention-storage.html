Vapor Barrier Bag Crating

What is a vapor barrier bag or VCI (Volatile Corrosion Inhibitors)? This is the protection between your items that are being shipped. High barrier bags are also used to preserve and protect products. This is done since they have a low oxygen transmittal rate, low moisture vapor transmission rate, and a high tensile strength, or puncture resistance, against airborne contaminants. The vapor barrier bag is a line of defense for our customers highly valued goods that will protect these items from corrosion and rust. There are a few different types of VCI, one is a heat sealed bag, heat sealed shrink or, a coated paper.

ISPM-15 Heat Treated Wood Shipping

What this means to you is that we meet all ISPM 15 standards for the heat treatment of wood materials and packaging components that must attain a minimum core temperature of 56 degrees Celsius (133 degree Fahrenheit) for at least thirty minutes. The heat treatment certification program of the U.S. is administered by the American Lumber Standards Committee. Las Vegas Crating & Logistics packing, crating, and shipping have accredited heat treating facilities and services meet or exceed the International Standards for Phytosanitry Measures & Guidelines for the Regulating of Wood Packaging Materials in International Trade.
