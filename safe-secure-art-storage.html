What To Consider When Choosing an Art Storage Warehouse

Adjustable Storage Options
1. Climate Control

Maintaining the right temperature and humidity levels is critical for preserving artworks. Many art storage facilities use climate-controlled environments to prevent fluctuations that can cause cracking, warping, and other damage.

2. Security

Security measures like access control, surveillance cameras, and alarm systems are crucial to prevent theft and vandalism. High-value artworks may require specialized security arrangements.

3. Lighting

Artworks should be stored in spaces with minimal exposure to natural and artificial light to prevent fading and other light-related damage. When lighting is necessary, it should be carefully controlled.

4. Warehouse Location

Short or long term storage in your future? Consider the location of the fulfillment center and their shipping carrier options.

Full Service Packing and Crating
1. Packing and Crating

Artworks should be packed and crated using materials that protect them from physical damage during transportation and storage. These materials should be archival and non-reactive to ensure they don't harm the art over time.

2. Handling

Proper handling procedures should be followed to minimize the risk of damage during the placement, removal, or movement of artworks. This often involves using gloves and other protective measures.

3. Shelving and Racking

Art storage facilities may use specialized shelving and racking systems designed to support various types of artworks safely and efficiently.

4. Cataloging and Inventory

Each artwork should be cataloged, and a detailed inventory should be maintained. This helps track the location and condition of each piece, making it easier to manage and access the collection.

Safe and Secure
1. Documentation

Proper documentation of artworks, including condition reports and provenance records, is essential for insurance purposes and to track any changes in the artwork's condition over time.

2. Pest Control

Art storage facilities should have measures in place to prevent infestations by pests, such as insects or rodents, which can damage artworks.

3. Transportation

When artworks need to be moved, they should be transported using specialized methods and vehicles to ensure their safety. This may include climate-controlled transport for sensitive pieces.

4. Conservation and Restoration

Artworks in storage may require periodic conservation or restoration work to address any deterioration or damage. Having access to professional conservators is important for maintaining the art's condition.
