Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Las Vegas Warehouse ("Las Vegas Warehouse," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit our website, request a quote, or use our storage, fulfillment, and 3PL logistics services.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact information you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, and mailing or shipping address.
  • Business information related to storage, shipping, or fulfillment requests, including inventory details and service preferences.
  • Communications you send us through contact forms, email, or phone, including the content of those messages.
  • Website usage data, such as pages visited and general browser/device information, collected automatically through standard web technologies.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to quote requests, inquiries, and applications
  • Provide, operate, and improve our storage, fulfillment, and logistics services
  • Communicate with you about your account, shipments, or inventory
  • Maintain the security and integrity of our facility and website
  • Comply with legal obligations

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate our business (e.g., IT hosting, email delivery, and logistics partners), under obligations to protect your information
  • Carriers and shipping partners as necessary to fulfill storage, shipping, or delivery requests
  • Legal or regulatory authorities when required by law or to protect our rights, property, or safety

Cookies & Website Data

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support basic functionality and understand how visitors use our site. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect certain site features.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect the information we collect, both for your data and the physical assets stored at our facility. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, maintain accurate business and shipping records, and comply with legal, accounting, or reporting requirements.

Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. To make a request, contact us using the information below.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for business and adult use and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
